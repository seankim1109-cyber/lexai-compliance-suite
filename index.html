HIGH RISK
Machine Translation

Verified Legal Interpretation
"§ 21 credit is strictly limited to Auslandsvermögen (foreign-sited assets) and applies only where no tax treaty governs. Where the US-Germany Estate Tax Treaty applies, treaty credit provisions under Art. 11 supersede § 21 and impose a proportionate credit calculation methodology."

Critical Gap: Translation implies a broad unilateral credit; in practice the treaty Art. 11 mechanism is mandatory and uses a different proportionality formula. Failing to apply treaty credit method could result in overpayment or understatement of residual liability.

This compliance analysis identifies significant structural deficiencies in the machine-translated excerpt of the German Erbschaftsteuer- und Schenkungsteuergesetz (ErbStG) as applied to cross-border estate transfers between Germany and the United States.
